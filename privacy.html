Controlled beta / Privacy

Your story belongs to you.

Draft for beta review — revised November 6, 2026; not yet in effect.

This notice describes the WONDER application and its controlled adult beta. Your private Mirror and journal are separate from the Connection Portrait you choose to share.

Information used by the application

Account information includes your email, username, chosen name, private birth date, broad location, and authentication records. Passwords are handled by our authentication service. If you add a passkey, WONDER stores its public credential and device label, never your fingerprint or Face ID data.

When you use discovery, we store your answers, saved progress, assessment results, generated Mirror, corrections and their history, and permission choices. Optional journal entries, tags, reflections, and reviewed memories are associated with your account. Your gender, who you wish to meet, relationship preferences, and anything you choose to write may reveal sensitive information about you.

Connection features use the portrait text, interests, and photograph you provide, your availability and preferences, introduction decisions, messages, and meeting plans. We also retain support reports and records needed to operate the service, enforce security limits, and handle account deletion.

How your information is used

Discovery answers are processed to prepare an authored, personalized Mirror. Introductions use reciprocal requirements, eligible assessment information you permit, and approved shared interests. These are interpretive product rules, not validated predictions of chemistry or relationship outcomes.

The current introduction rules do not score your journal, messages, private date feedback, saved memory text, or raw correction text. Your correction choices can exclude assessment dimensions from consideration. You can review your Mirror, correct interpretations, pause new introductions, and change permissions in Account. Pausing new introductions does not by itself close an existing mutual connection.

What another member can see

An eligible introduction can see the Connection Portrait you approve for sharing, including your chosen name, age, broad city, photograph, relationship intention, and shared portrait details. This portrait is visible before mutual interest. Introduction explanations can describe shared interests and participation preferences. Your full birth date, private Mirror, journal, corrections, and private reflections are not included in the shared portrait. Anything you put into your portrait or send in a message is shared with its recipient.

Messaging opens only after both people express interest. Messages and meeting plans, including any location, budget, or access needs you enter, are then visible to that connection.

Blocking closes contact within WONDER. It cannot remove information someone has already seen, copied, or saved outside the application.

Service access and providers

Private from other members does not mean inaccessible to WONDER. The operations interface gives authorized operators access to member names and cities, portrait photographs, support reports, cohort membership, and pending deletion records. Privileged service administration can access other stored records. The service is not end-to-end encrypted.

Operators review portrait photographs and approve membership in the introduction cohort. You approve your portrait text for sharing. Individual introductions are generated by the application's rules; they do not each receive a separate human approval. Photo review and cohort approval are not identity checks or guarantees about another person's conduct.

Vercel hosts the application and handles requests to it. Supabase provides authentication, the database, and photograph storage. The current Supabase database is configured in Canada. Pages request fonts from Google Fonts. These services process information for their functions, including technical request information. Supabase Auth initiates confirmation and password-reset emails; delivery also involves the email service configured for the project.

The application also has a place-search service using OpenStreetMap Nominatim. If that service is used, the search text is sent from WONDER's server to Nominatim. The current beta's city field is entered directly.

Cookies and browser storage

WONDER uses cookies for sign-in sessions, account confirmation, and password recovery. The demonstration uses storage in your browser tab for its progress and anything you enter there. The offline feature caches a public offline page and styling, rather than member API responses. Downloads, screenshots, and calendar exports saved on your device remain under your control.

AI and optional memories

Live conversational Mirror is not enabled for this beta. Saved Mirrors use authored text and assessment rules; corrections, private journals, and introductions work independently of a conversational model. The current application keeps research permission off and does not enable member-data research or model training.

You can choose to save and remove optional memories. The application records a separate introduction-use permission, but the current introduction rules do not use memory text to rank candidates. Any future conversational AI, research, or model-training feature requires a separate review of its data use, provider, retention, and permissions before it is offered.

Your choices and deletion

You can edit your portrait, withdraw processing or matching permission, pause new introductions, remove optional memories, and delete journal entries. Withdrawing permission is not the same as deleting your account or all previously stored records. Deleting a journal entry also removes memories linked to that entry. Correction history and earlier assessment records can remain until account deletion.

The Account export includes your private journey and selected account, assessment, report, journal, and feedback records. It does not include all information held by the service: connection messages and plans, photo files, and operational or provider records are not included in that download.

Removing or replacing a photograph withdraws it from your current portrait; it does not erase the stored file. Older photographs can remain accessible to you and authorized operators until account deletion or separate removal. The current application does not schedule automatic deletion of those older files.

Account deletion requires password confirmation. Once the deletion request is recorded, the application denies further account access and attempts to remove stored portrait files, the authentication account, and linked private records. Deleting either participant's account also removes the stored connection, including its messages and plans. If cleanup cannot finish, the request remains pending for an operator to retry. A failed request that was not recorded does not establish that access was withdrawn.

A deletion receipt containing the account identifier, request time, status, and completion time remains; it has no automatic expiry in the current application. Reports you submitted are removed with your account. A report submitted by someone else can remain after references to your account and connection are removed; its written text may still identify you. Closing a report does not itself delete it. The current application has no scheduled expiry for these support records.

Security rate-limit records use keyed hashes of request identifiers. Expired records are cleaned up during later requests, not by an automatic timer. Account deletion does not directly erase provider service logs or any backup copies. Their existence, retention, and removal depend on the services and backup arrangements actually in use.

Adults and support

WONDER is for adults aged 18 or older. Email confirmation verifies access to an inbox, not a person's identity. Report a concern from a connection's support controls; blocking does not require a report. Support is not an emergency service and no immediate response time is promised.

A public contact for privacy requests and help without signing in must be confirmed before this draft takes effect. Avoid sending passwords, full journal entries, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Changes to this notice

This controlled beta may change as the service develops. Material changes to data use, providers, or enabled intelligence features will require an updated notice.
