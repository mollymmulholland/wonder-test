Controlled beta / Privacy

Your story belongs to you.

Notice prepared November 4, 2026

This notice describes the WONDER application and its controlled adult beta. Your private Mirror and journal are separate from the Connection Portrait you choose to share.

Information used by the application

Account information includes your email, username, chosen name, private birth date, broad location, and authentication records. Passwords are handled by our authentication service. If you add a passkey, WONDER stores its public credential and device label, never your fingerprint or Face ID data.

When you use discovery, we store your answers, saved progress, generated Mirror, corrections, and permission choices. Optional journal entries, tags, reflections, and reviewed memories are associated with your account. Relationship preferences may reveal sensitive information about you.

Connection features use the portrait text, interests, and photograph you provide, your availability and preferences, introduction decisions, messages, and meeting plans. We also retain support reports and records needed to operate the service, enforce security limits, and handle account deletion.

How your information is used

Discovery answers are processed to prepare an authored, personalized Mirror. Introductions use reciprocal requirements, eligible assessment information you permit, and approved shared interests. These are interpretive product rules, not validated predictions of chemistry or relationship outcomes.

Your journal, messages, private date feedback, and raw correction text are not automatically used as matching scores. You can review your Mirror, correct interpretations, pause introductions, and change permissions in Account.

What another member can see

An eligible introduction can see your approved Connection Portrait, including your chosen name, age, broad city, approved photograph, and shared portrait details. Your full birth date, private Mirror, journal, corrections, and private reflections are not included. Messaging opens only after both people express interest; messages and meeting plans are then visible to that connection.

Blocking closes contact within WONDER. It cannot remove information someone has already seen, copied, or saved outside the application.

Service access and providers

Private from other members does not mean inaccessible to WONDER. Authorized service operators can access records when needed to run, support, review, and secure the service. Portrait photographs are subject to human review before introduction availability is approved. The service is not end-to-end encrypted.

Vercel hosts the application. Supabase supplies authentication, database, and photograph storage. Pages also request fonts from Google Fonts. These services receive the information necessary to provide their functions, including technical request information. Account emails pass through the configured email delivery provider.

AI and optional memories

Live conversational Mirror is not connected in this release. The saved Mirror, corrections, private journal, and introduction rules work independently of a conversational model. Research and model-improvement use is not enabled in the application.

If conversational AI is introduced, its provider and processing practices must be disclosed before use. The existing design sends only your conversation and material you explicitly select. Saving a memory requires separate review; permission to use that memory for introductions is a separate choice. Journals are not read automatically.

Your choices and deletion

You can edit your portrait, withdraw permissions, pause availability, remove optional memories, delete journal entries, and export your private data in Account. Removing or replacing a portrait photograph withdraws it from your current portrait; older stored photographs can remain until account deletion.

Account deletion requires password confirmation. The application withdraws access and requests removal of active private records and stored portraits. If removal cannot complete, it records a pending request for an operator to retry. A limited deletion receipt remains to prevent access during and after removal. Provider backups and service logs follow their own retention schedules and are not erased immediately by an in-app deletion request.

Adults and support

WONDER is for adults aged 18 or older. Email confirmation verifies access to an inbox, not a person's identity. Report a concern from a connection's support controls; blocking does not require a report. Support is not an emergency service and no immediate response time is promised.

For privacy requests or help when you cannot sign in, reply to your WONDER beta invitation or beta correspondence. Avoid sending passwords, full journal entries, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Changes to this notice

This controlled beta may change as the service develops. Material changes to data use, providers, or enabled intelligence features will require an updated notice.
