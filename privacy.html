Privacy notice

Your story belongs to you.

Effective August 18, 2026

What we collect

When you apply for Wonder's private beta, we may collect information you choose to provide, including your first name, email address, city, relationship intent, and written responses about what you would like others to understand about you. We may also collect basic referral and campaign information associated with how you reached the site.

How we use it

We use this information to manage the Wonder private beta, contact applicants about early access, understand where interest is developing, conduct product research, improve the experience, and evaluate potential testing communities.

How we protect it

Beta application data is stored using third-party infrastructure providers selected to support secure data handling. We do not sell your beta application information.

Who this is for

The private beta application is intended only for people age 18 or older.

Your choices

You may ask to have your beta application information corrected or deleted. You may also opt out of future beta communications at any time.

Changes

Wonder is an early-stage product, and this notice may evolve as the product and our data practices develop. Material changes will be reflected on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Wonder through the email address used in our beta communications.
